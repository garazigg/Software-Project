## 1. What went well (keep)

- We held **five conversations with Jon** (Meetings 1 to 5), each one logged with its date,
  questions and answers in `docs/client/client-interviews.pdf`, and Jon confirmed the
  requirements CR-01 to CR-12 in Meeting 5.
- **Roles were clear from the start**: the Belbin test gave each member a role, the gaps (Plant,
  Implementer) were named with an action, and every member signed the team charter by 19 November.
- **GitHub was ready before any code**: repository and folders, issue templates, project board
  with five columns, milestones for three sprints, and 12 user stories plus 2 tasks as issues
  (#16–#29), each traced to a confirmed requirement.
- Every document was written **in English**, with an `AI use: …` line when AI helped.
- When the scope changed, we **updated every document the same day** (vision, backlog, risks,
  board guide, README, charter).

## 2. What did not (stop)

- **We wrote the backlog before the scope was stable.** The 12 stories of 17 November were based
  on the Meeting 4 requirements; Meeting 5 (19 November) changed the scope to four steps, so the
  stories and their issues were thrown away and rewritten in one evening.
- **Issues were deleted instead of closed**, so their history and comments were lost and the new
  issues start at #16.
- **The new user stories went into GitHub before Olga reviewed them and Ali ordered them**, which
  is the opposite of the order in our charter (write → review → approve → issues).
- **Our documents contradicted each other**: who assigns the tasks (charter vs. board guide), the
  repository name (`Software-Proyect` in the charter), links to `vision.md` while the vision was a
  PDF, and the review date of S06.
- **Most GitHub work fell on one person** (repository, issues, board, README, risks), because the
  rest of the team is not yet comfortable with GitHub (risk R-02).
- **Estimated and real hours were not logged in the issues as we worked**, so the hours table of
  the sprint backlog had to be filled in afterwards. <confirm or remove>
